# Mistry — India-first visual direction

This is a product and brand requirement for all future UI screens, marketing images, illustrations and photography.

## People and faces
- Whenever a real or generated human face is shown, use Indian people only. Do not use foreign-looking stock-photo models or generic western lifestyle imagery.
- Represent the diversity of Indian customers and service professionals: different regions, skin tones, ages and genders, without caricatures or stereotypes.
- For technician imagery, show believable Indian home-service professionals in practical, neat workwear and realistic working environments.
- For customer imagery, use natural, everyday Indian homes and familiar settings rather than luxury or foreign-looking interiors unless a brief specifically calls for them.
- Keep expressions and poses natural, respectful and authentic. Avoid presenting any community, region or occupation as a stereotype.
- Use only properly licensed or newly generated image assets approved for the product.

## Current prototype treatment
The current UI deliberately uses initials and icon illustrations instead of portrait photographs. This keeps the prototype lightweight and avoids introducing non-local stock photography. If portraits are added later, the requirements above are mandatory.

## Local product details
- Use Indian currency formatting (₹), Indian phone-number formats and Indian place names in demo content.
- Keep the visual language suitable for an Indian home-services marketplace.
